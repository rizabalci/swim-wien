Live occupancy comes from the City of Vienna Bäderampel open-data layer (SCHWIMMBADOGD), the exact same feed wien.gv.at uses. It refreshes through the day and also carries a 3-day outlook. Pools run by other operators (Stadthallenbad, Stadionbad, Therme Wien, Badeschiff) are in the feed but the city does not publish occupancy for them — those show grey.
Hours & prices were checked against official sources on 29 July 2026 and reflect the late-July/August 2026 schedule. Vienna's pools change hours constantly for maintenance, swim courses and heatwaves — the "official page" link on each card is always the truth. Outdoor pools can close from 15:00 in bad weather (Gänsehäufel from 17:00).
Photos are freely licensed images from Wikimedia Commons — 30 of the 55 places have one, and each caption credits the photographer and licence and links to the source. The remaining 25 are hotels, gyms and small district baths for which no freely licensed photograph exists, so they show a satellite view instead (© Mapbox © Maxar), with a link to the operators' own photos on Google Maps.
